What it does

Fair scale card

Type one log and see every rule next to each other, with the percent between them. The quickest way to settle a price talk at the landing. Free.

Tallies that stay yours

As many tallies and logs as you like. Edit any line, see the totals, export CSV or PDF and back up to one file. None of it is kept behind Pro.

Made for the yard

Big keys for gloved hands, the last length stays filled in, and a high-contrast theme for full sun. No signal needed at any point.

Firewood stacks

Full cord, face cord (rick), stacked cubic metres (Raummeter, ster) and loose measure, with the solid wood in each and the source of every factor.

Sawn lumber

Board feet and cubic metres for boards and beams in the sizes you enter, inches or millimetres.
