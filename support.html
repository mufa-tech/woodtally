Common questions

How do I restore Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the Pro screen or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Google account you bought Pro with. A purchase made with a Play promo code, or a payment that cleared late, shows up by itself the next time you open the app.

How does the trial work?

You start it yourself from the Pro screen. For 7 days every Pro tool is open. The trial runs on your phone only and never turns into a charge, so there is nothing to cancel. When it ends, Pro tools go back to preview, and your tallies, edits, totals and exports stay free.

Why do Doyle, Scribner and International ¼ give different numbers?

They were built on different ideas of how much lumber a log holds.

  • Doyle takes 4 inches off the diameter for slabs and squares the rest. Small logs scale low, very large logs scale high.
  • Scribner comes from drawings of 1-inch boards inside a circle, with a ¼-inch kerf and no taper. Scribner Decimal C rounds those figures to tens. It is a table, so Woodtally uses the USFS table and not an approximation.
  • International ¼ adds a taper of ½ inch per 4 feet and allows for kerf and shrinkage. It is closest to what a modern mill saws.
  • Cubic rules (Huber, Festmeter, kübaj) measure wood volume, not boards.

The fair scale card shows them side by side, with the rule, the table and the rounding next to every number. The board feet calculator on this site explains the gap in more detail.

How does rounding work?

Diameter rounding is your choice: drop the fraction (default), nearest inch or nearest ½ inch. The choice is printed on every ticket. Each rule then rounds the way its table does: Doyle to whole board feet, Scribner Decimal C to tens, International ¼ to the nearest 5. Metric rules follow their own practice, for example the German RVR rounds the diameter down to whole centimetres and the volume to two decimals.

Which rule should I use?

The one in your contract, or the one your buyer scales by. Some states name a rule for certain sales. Ask before you agree a price per thousand, and check a few logs on more than one rule.

How do I back up and move to a new phone?

In Settings, tap Back up everything. You get one file with every tally and setting. Keep it in Drive, mail it to yourself or copy it to a USB stick. On the new phone, install Woodtally, tap Restore from a backup and pick the file.

Can I open my tallies in Excel?

Yes. Export a tally as CSV and open it in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice. PDF tickets can be printed or shared from the same menu. Both are free.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works without signal. Only buying or restoring Pro talks to Google Play.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Woodtally is on Android for now.
