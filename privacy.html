Privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: Woodtally for Android (com.mufatech.woodtally) · Publisher: MUFATECH

Short version: Woodtally collects nothing and shares nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. What you enter stays on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no sign-in, no analytics or crash reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking identifiers. We do not receive your tallies, prices, customer names, settings or any information about how you use the app.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your device, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We have no copy and no way to see it. Uninstalling the app removes this data from the phone.

If Android's own device backup is switched on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's files in the backup it keeps in your Google account. That backup is a system feature controlled by you and Google; we cannot read it.

Backups and exports

Backup files, CSV files and PDF tickets are created on your phone when you ask for them. You choose where they go through Android's share menu or file picker (for example Drive, e-mail or a USB stick). We never receive them. Once you send a file to someone or to a service, their privacy terms apply to that copy.

Purchases

Woodtally Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that Pro was bought, so it can switch Pro on. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

The 7-day trial runs on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only connection is the one Google Play Billing makes when you buy or restore Pro.

Permissions

The app asks for no runtime permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access. The only entry in its manifest is Google Play's billing permission, added by the billing library. Files are opened only when you pick one yourself in Android's file picker.

Children

Woodtally is a tool for timber and firewood work and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. The calculators run in your browser and send nothing. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers for them. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the effective date above.
